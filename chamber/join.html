NP Membership Benefits

The NP Membership is designed for qualifying non-profit organizations.

  • Free chamber membership
  • Business directory listing
  • Access to community events
  • Networking opportunities

Bronze Membership Benefits

Bronze membership provides essential support for developing businesses.

  • Everything included with NP benefits
  • Enhanced directory listing
  • Business training opportunities
  • Access to selected chamber events
  • Member event discounts

Silver Membership Benefits

Silver membership provides additional visibility and promotional opportunities.

  • Everything included with Bronze benefits
  • Priority business directory placement
  • Additional training opportunities
  • Discounts on chamber events
  • Social media promotion
  • Business networking opportunities

Gold Membership Benefits

Gold membership provides premium exposure, networking, and promotional opportunities.

  • Everything included with Silver benefits
  • Premium directory placement
  • Homepage business spotlight opportunities
  • Priority event sponsorship opportunities
  • Premium advertising opportunities
  • Special networking events
  • Exclusive training opportunities
