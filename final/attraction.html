A Destination Rich in Heritage

Grand-Bassam is a coastal town in southeastern Côte d'Ivoire. Its historic district preserves buildings and urban spaces that reflect the town's architectural, administrative, and commercial past.

The Historic Town of Grand-Bassam is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Its heritage includes historic architecture and the continuing cultural life of the local community.
